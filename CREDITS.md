# Credits

Kaze Drift generates most of its textures, sounds and music in code. The car models below are third-party assets used under their licenses; CC-BY models are credited exactly as their licenses require. The same list is shown in-game under **Settings → Credits**.

|---|---|---|---|
| [three.js](https://threejs.org) | three.js authors | MIT | Rendering, math, Sky shader, post-processing passes (EffectComposer, UnrealBloom, GTAO, SMAA, Output) |
| [Chakra Petch](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Chakra+Petch) via @fontsource | Cadson Demak | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | Numbers, HUD, UI |

## Names

All car, brand, sponsor, wheel, tire and place names are fictional (see `src/data/names.json`). Any resemblance to a real product is by design only in silhouette and spirit.
